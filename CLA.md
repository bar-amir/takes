# Takes Contributor License Agreement

Version 1.0 · 2026-10-26

Thank you for contributing to Takes. This agreement explains what rights you give Bar Amir (the "Maintainer") when you contribute. You keep the copyright in your work. You give the Maintainer a broad license so the project can stay open source today and keep the option of being released under other terms later, including as a paid or closed-source app.

You accept this agreement by replying to your first pull request with this exact sentence: "I have read the Takes CLA (version 1.0) and I agree to it." A bot records your signature (your GitHub username and the time) in `signatures.json` on the repository's `cla-signatures` branch, and it covers all your future pull requests.

## 1. Definitions

- **"Contribution"** means any code, documentation, design, text or other material that you submit to the Takes project, for example by pull request, issue, patch or message, and that the Maintainer accepts into the project.
- **"You"** means the person submitting the Contribution. If you contribute on behalf of an employer or other organisation, "You" also means that organisation, and you confirm you are allowed to sign for it.

You grant the Maintainer, and anyone who receives software distributed by the Maintainer, a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, free of charge, royalty-free and irrevocable license to use, reproduce, modify, prepare derivative works of, publicly display, publicly perform, sublicense and distribute your Contributions and derivative works of them.

## 3. Relicensing

The Maintainer may distribute your Contributions under the project's current license (MIT) or under any other license or terms the Maintainer chooses, including proprietary or commercial terms, without asking you again and without paying you. Versions already released under the MIT license stay available under the MIT license.

## 4. Patent license

You grant the Maintainer, and anyone who receives software distributed by the Maintainer, a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, free of charge, royalty-free and irrevocable patent license to make, use, sell, offer to sell, import and otherwise transfer your Contributions. This covers only patent claims that you can license and that would be infringed by your Contribution alone or combined with the project. If you start patent litigation claiming that the project or a Contribution infringes a patent, the patent licenses you received under this agreement for the project end on the date that litigation is filed.

## 5. What you confirm

- Each Contribution is your original work, or you have the right to submit it under this agreement. If it includes someone else's work, you say so clearly and include its license.
- If your employer or anyone else has rights in your Contributions (for example under an employment agreement), you have their permission to contribute under this agreement, or they have waived those rights.
- To your knowledge, your Contributions don't infringe anyone else's rights.
- You will tell the Maintainer if any of this stops being true.

## 6. No obligations

The Maintainer doesn't have to accept, use or credit any Contribution. Except as stated in section 5, you provide your Contributions "as is", without warranties or conditions of any kind. You don't have to provide support for them.

## 7. Moral rights

To the extent the law allows, you agree not to assert moral rights (such as the right to object to changes) in a way that would stop the Maintainer from using your Contributions as this agreement allows. You may ask to be credited, and the Maintainer will make reasonable efforts to do so in the project's contributor list.

## 8. General

This agreement covers all your past and future Contributions to Takes. The Maintainer may publish a new version of this agreement. A new version applies only to Contributions made after you accept it.
