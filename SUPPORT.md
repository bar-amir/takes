# Getting help

Takes is free and maintained by one person in spare time. Here's where to go, and what to expect.

| You want to… | Go to |
|---|---|
| Learn the shortcuts and settings | [Using Takes](docs/usage.md) |
| Ask a question or get help with setup | [Discussions › Q&A](https://github.com/bar-amir/takes/discussions/categories/q-a) |
| Suggest an idea or share a preset | [Discussions › Ideas](https://github.com/bar-amir/takes/discussions/categories/ideas), or a [feature request](https://github.com/bar-amir/takes/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml) once it's concrete |
| Report something broken | [Bug report](https://github.com/bar-amir/takes/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml) |
| Report an app where Takes can't read or paste | [App compatibility report](https://github.com/bar-amir/takes/issues/new?template=app_compatibility.yml) |
| Report a security problem | Privately, as described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Never in a public issue. |

Before you post, search the [issues](https://github.com/bar-amir/takes/issues?q=is%3Aissue) and [discussions](https://github.com/bar-amir/takes/discussions). Someone may already have asked.

## Keep your writing private

Don't paste private drafts, messages or API keys into issues or discussions. Describe them instead ("a two-paragraph reply in Slack"). The field-reading log (`~/Library/Logs/Takes/focus.log`) records the app, the kind of text box and character counts, and for each paste whether it was sent or why it wasn't, never your text, so it's safe to share.

## What to expect

- Replies are best effort, usually within a week. There's no paid support.
- Bugs that affect many people, and security issues, come first.
- An issue labelled `needs info` gets a reminder after two weeks without a reply and closes a week later. Reply with the details at any time and it will be reopened.
- Only the latest release is supported. Update from the menu-bar icon › Check for Updates… before reporting a bug.

## Problems with Claude, ChatGPT or another provider

Takes uses your own account. Billing, plan limits, rate limits and account access are handled by the provider you picked (Anthropic, OpenAI and others), and Takes can't help with those.
